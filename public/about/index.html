About Hit the Line

Hit the Line is a reference for one question: how often does a team go over a line?

What we count

For every team we count the share of games that finished over a line on four things: corners, cards, total goals and both teams to score. The answer is given as k of n games, for example "over 9.5 corners in 9 of 13 games".

Lines

A line is a number. Over 9.5 corners means ten or more corners in the match, both teams combined. We show several lines per metric, home and away splits, first and second half, and the latest matches.

How a match is counted

Only completed matches of the current season (2026/27) count in Championship, Primeira Liga, Ligue 2, Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, 2. Bundesliga, Belgian Pro League. Totals are for both teams combined. A match with incomplete or inconsistent data, such as a missing corner count or half totals that do not add up to the final figure, is left out and the page says how many were excluded. A team needs a minimum number of usable games before a percentage is shown.

How often it is refreshed

Data is refreshed daily and every page shows the date of its data ("Data as of 2026-11-27").

Club crests

Small club crests appear next to team names so teams are easier to recognise. They belong to their clubs and are stored on this site. A club that wants its crest removed can ask us and we will replace it with a monogram the same day. A contact e-mail address will be added here.
